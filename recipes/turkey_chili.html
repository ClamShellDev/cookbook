Turkey Chili

Simple chili recipe. Serves well with corn bread or tortilla chips. Top with sliced jalapeno, sour cream, and cheese.

Ingredients

  • bell pepper – 1 med. chopped
  • black beans – 15 oz can
  • black pepper – 1/2 teaspoon
  • cayenne pepper – 1/4 teaspoon
  • chicken/veggie broth – 1 1/4 cups
  • chili powder – to taste (1-4 tablespoons)
  • crushed tomatoes – 28oz can
  • cumin – 2 teaspoons
  • fresh garlic – 3 cloves minced
  • ground turkey – 1 lb
  • kidney beans – 15 oz can
  • olive oil – 1/2 tablespoon
  • oregano – 1 teaspoon
  • salt – 1/2 teaspoon
  • sweet corn – 15 oz can
  • sweet potato – 1 med. diced
  • yellow onion – 1 med. diced

Steps

Step 1
Prepare Raw Ingredients (prep, 10 minutes)

Ingredients: sweet potato (1 med. diced), yellow onion (1 med. diced), bell pepper (1 med. chopped), fresh garlic (3 cloves minced)

Prepare raw ingredients for later addition to chili.

Preheat Large Pot (prep, 5 minutes)

Ingredients: olive oil (1/2 tablespoon)

Preheart a large, oiled pot on medium heat.

Step 2
Saute Aromatics (cook, 5-8 minutes)

Add raw ingredients (onion, potato, pepper & garlic) to preheated pot and saute until fragrant.

Step 3
Season Chili Base (combine, 5 minutes)

Ingredients: chili powder (to taste (1-4 tablespoons)), cumin (2 teaspoons), oregano (1 teaspoon), cayenne pepper (1/4 teaspoon), salt (1/2 teaspoon), black pepper (1/2 teaspoon)

Add seasonings to pot and stir.

Add Turkey (cook, 10 minutes)

Ingredients: ground turkey (1 lb)

Add turkey (or ground substitute) to pot. Cook until no pink/red remains.

Step 4
Final Additions and Reduction (cook, 40-50 minutes)

Ingredients: crushed tomatoes (28oz can), chicken/veggie broth (1 1/4 cups), kidney beans (15 oz can), black beans (15 oz can), sweet corn (15 oz can)

Add final ingredients. Bring pot to a boil then reduce heat to simmer and cover. Cook until reduced/thickened.
